Equal parts Malbec and Pinot Noir from a single stony, calcareous vineyard in Gualtallary, fermented whole-cluster by carbonic maceration. Juicy and high-toned, with herbal and faintly smoky notes, soft tannins and an energetic, stony finish. Made to be served with a light chill.
