Criolla Chica from 80-year-old pergola vines beside the Los Patos river, at 1,500 metres in the Calingasta Valley. Wild strawberry and cherry with floral lift and a touch of spice. Light to medium-bodied, vibrant acidity, gentle tannins and a clear mineral line.
