Torrontés grown between 1,700 and 2,300 metres in the Upper Calchaquí Valley — among the highest vineyards in the world. Rose petal, grapefruit and a spicy hint on the nose; fresh, round and generous in the mouth with a long, elegant finish. Dry, but its aromatics read as gently sweet.
