Sémillon from own-rooted vines planted in the late 1960s in two historic zones of Río Negro. White flowers, orange peel, white peach and beeswax on the nose; the palate is tart and complex with green apple, lemon zest and herbal hints — structured, high in acid, with honeyed notes and a lingering saline finish.
